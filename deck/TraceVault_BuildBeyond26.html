01 · Analysis of the Problem Statement
Understanding the Problem
Problem Statement · PS 3.2AI-Powered Digital Evidence Intelligence Platform

Victims of harassment, fraud and stalking struggle to organise evidence scattered across screenshots, emails, chats, audio and documents, and rebuilding a coherent timeline by hand is extremely time-consuming.

Build a platform that uses OCR, NLP and speech-to-text to extract evidence, detect duplicates, rebuild timelines, flag inconsistencies, write cited summaries, map people and events, and keep every file tamper-evident.

Context

Harassment, fraud and stalking now happen online; the proof lives in chats, screenshots and voice notes.

Who Is Affected

Victims and families, plus the lawyers, NGOs and police cyber cells who build their cases.

Root Causes

Proof is scattered across formats, sorting it is slow and manual, and edits are hard to rule out.

Why Now

BSA §63 requires a hash value for every digital record, upheld by the Supreme Court in 2026.
